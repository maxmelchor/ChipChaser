How Chip makes projections

Chip isn’t just guessing. Under the hood, each projection is built from:

  • Multi-season PPR production (rookie year → 2024)
  • Age curve into the 2025 season
  • Coverage tier of the opposing CB (elite / good / average)
  • CB body type & play style (weight, height, press vs zone, archetype)
  • Historical splits vs similar CB builds & specific corners where we have data

Chip then outputs a 2025 neutral PPR per game baseline, adjusts it for the Week X CB matchup, and attaches a confidence score based on sample size. Volatility reflects how boom/bust a player’s usage profile is.

In this prototype, everything runs locally with curated sample data for Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, and DeVonta Smith — but the same engine can plug into a real stats pipeline.
