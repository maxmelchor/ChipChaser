What ChipChaser actually does for you

Under the hood, ChipChaser is a real-time engine watching every snap, every league transaction, and every projection shift — then boiling that chaos into a tiny list of "do this now" moves.

Real-time projection engine

Ingests multiple projection sources, injury reports, and line movement to keep your player outlooks live — not stale by Sunday morning.

Sit/Start & trade explanations

Every recommendation comes with a human-readable breakdown: matchup, usage trends, volatility, and risk tolerance.

ESPN + Sleeper sync

How it works

The live app will plug directly into your leagues. This demo shows a small slice of the decision engine running in your browser with sample data.

  1. Connect your ESPN or Sleeper leagues.
  2. We sync your rosters, matchups, and scoring.
  3. You ask questions in plain English — sit/start, waivers, trades.
  4. ChipChaser answers with context and a clear recommendation.

FAQ

Is this connected to real leagues yet?

This demo runs entirely in your browser with mock data. The production version will offer secure ESPN and Sleeper integrations.

Is this using "real AI" or just rules?

The goal is a hybrid system: hard math for projections and constraints, plus a language layer to explain decisions in English you actually understand.

Can I break this?

Please try. The whole point of this prototype is to see how people actually ask questions and where the decision engine needs to be sharper.
